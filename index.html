Why it matters

Why Clarksville businesses need a real website

Clarksville is one of the fastest-growing cities in Tennessee, and a lot of that growth is new families, including military families rotating through Fort Campbell, who don't know the town yet. They don't ask a neighbor. They search on their phone.

If all they find is an old Facebook page or a listing with no hours, they call the next business on the list. A clear, fast website tells them who you are, what you do, where you are and how to reach you, in about five seconds.

  • Get found on Google and Maps for “near me” searches.
  • Look established to newcomers who have never heard of you.
  • Answer the basics (hours, prices, location) without the phone ringing.
  • Own your presence instead of relying only on social media.

FAQ

Common questions

What does the $1,000 cover?

Design and build of a custom site (typically up to 5 pages: home, services, about, gallery or menu, contact), mobile optimization, contact form, Google Map and hours, basic on-page SEO, and setting up hosting and your domain.

Are there monthly fees?

No monthly fee to me. You'll pay your domain registrar directly (usually about $10–$20 a year). Many small sites can be hosted free or very cheaply. Optional updates and maintenance can be arranged if you want them.

How long does it take?

Most sites are ready to launch in one to two weeks, depending on how quickly I get your photos, logo and text.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site files are yours.

What if I already have a website?

If your current site is slow, outdated or hard to use on a phone, I can rebuild it for the same flat price and move your domain over.

How do I pay?

Typically half to start and half at launch. Payment details are confirmed in your quote.
